Quick guide · fabric

Capture truer fabric colors

Follow these steps to get a closer match to the Sanzo Wada colors.

  1. Prepare the fabric: lay it flat without folds or shiny areas. Choose a uniform area away from seams and prints.
  2. Use soft light: prefer indirect daylight; avoid direct sunlight, flash and mixed lighting.
  3. Limit camera processing: turn off filters and portrait/night modes; disable HDR if possible, and lock exposure and white balance where supported.
  4. Include a neutral gray card: place it in the same plane and light as the fabric, free from glare and shadows.
  5. Calibrate and sample: after taking a photo, tap “Select gray card” and its center. Then tap a uniform fabric area, away from the card.
  6. For greater consistency: keep lighting constant and tap a uniform, well-lit area of fabric.

Note: gray-card calibration primarily reduces color casts. It cannot undo all smartphone processing or replace a textile spectrophotometer.
